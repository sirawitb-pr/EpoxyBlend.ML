Target & Hypothesis
Discover high-strength epoxy formulations (>35 MPa) through the blending of different epoxy monomers (up to 7 components) with different hardeners and ratios. The ML surrogate captures non-linear micro-phase separation synergies that linear mixture averages miss.
Step 1
1. LAB TESTING
• Blend Synthesis: Precise formulation compounding.
• ASTM D1002 Tensile Test: Lap shear strength evaluation on adherends.
• Data Logging: Measures raw shear strength (MPa).
Step 2
2. DATA UPDATING
• Monomer & Hardener Database: Curates pure physical, topological & Hansen properties.
• Experiment Database: Aggregates compounding runs and measured strengths.
Step 3
3. MODEL CREATION
• Physical Mixing Rules: Computes non-linear descriptors (viscosity, Ra, EEW).
• RF, XGBoost & GPR Ensembles: Surrogate modeling captures micro-phase synergy.
Step 4
4. PROPOSE NEXT EXP
• Virtual Simplex Screening: All-combination multi-component candidate search.
• Top 5 High-Adhesion Targets: Recommends formulations for next lab compounding.
Decision Gate
EVALUATION & TERMINATION
• NO (<35 MPa): Add data to Stage 2, retrain models, loop again.
• YES (≥35 MPa): 🏆 TARGET SUCCESS! Formulation qualified for pilot scale-up and industrial transfer.
